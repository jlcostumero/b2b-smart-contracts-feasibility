## [v1.3] - 2026-10-20
### Final Review Refinements & Publication Release
- **Categorical Heuristic Column**: Replaced binary Heuristic 1 Yes/No with a full categorical classification column (*Primary Failure Heuristic*: H1: Free/Bundled Incumbent [25 hypotheses, 47.2%]; H2: Legal/AML Statutory Vector [23 hypotheses, 43.4%]; H3: Physical Sensor Oracle Fallacy [5 hypotheses, 9.4%]), providing complete data traceability for all three causal heuristics.
- **Prudent Characterization of the 18 Viable Hypotheses**: Rephrased findings from \'positive unit contribution margins\' to \'successfully cleared the threshold hurdle gates of Filters 1 through 4\', emphasizing that their failure is operational redundancy against zero-marginal-cost Web2 platforms rather than continuous econometric solvency.
- **Physical Sensor Oracle Decoupling**: Clarified in Section 2.2 and rubric that Filter 2 assesses on-chain L2 execution, while the physical oracle vulnerability (Heuristic 3) is treated as a qualitative causal vector whose commercial impact manifests at Filter 5 against SCADA hardware enclaves.
- **Methodological Limitations & LLM Agent Personas**: Explicitly noted the ordinal qualitative nature of numerical scores (1.0 to 10.0), acknowledged shared foundation model priors among LLM personas (DeepSeek-V3 / Claude 3.5 Sonnet under Google Antigravity), and published all prompt templates in .agents/skills/.
- **ASX CHESS Review Citation & Details**: Cited official Accenture title (*CHESS Replacement Application Delivery Review: Summary Report*) with official ASX URL, adjusting technical findings to participant state determinism and high-volume settlement batch throughput.
- **Table 2 Frontier Directions**: Labeled separate columns for *Validation Criterion* and *Falsification Condition*.
- **Highlighted 18 Hypotheses in Abstract & Conclusions**: Prominently featured the 18 viable-but-redundant propositions in both the Abstract and Conclusions as the central strategic finding of the research.
